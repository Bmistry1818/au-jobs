Data sources and dates

    Employment, earnings and growth figures are for ANZSCO occupations, as published by Jobs and Skills Australia and licensed under CC BY 4.0. Demand figures count online job advertisements, not all vacancies, so they show momentum rather than headcount.

    AI exposure scores are model-generated estimates of how much AI is likely to reshape each occupation. They are not forecasts of job losses, and they have not yet been checked against expert labels. Code and methodology: github.com/Bmistry1818/au-jobs.
